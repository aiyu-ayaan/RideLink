# RideLink brand

## The mark: "Closed loop"
A thick ring with one opening at the upper right, completed by a **spark**: a dot of the ring's own weight,
sitting on the ring's path with an even gap to both ends. Phone and scooter, one circuit.
One sentence: *the spark closes the loop.*

Construction (256 grid, `logo_geometry.py` is the source of truth; never redraw by hand):
- ring centreline radius 88, weight 40, round ends, centred (128, 128)
- opening centred on -45 deg; each end 33 deg from it (10-unit air gap to the spark)
- spark radius 20 (= half the ring weight), on the ring centreline at -45 deg

## Colour
| Name | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|
| RideLink Red | `#B3261E` | brand colour, launcher background, ring on light. The splash follows the app theme instead (`splash_colors.xml`: surface + primary, dynamic on Android 12+) |
| Spark Amber | `#FFB82E` | the spark on red or dark backgrounds only |
| Ink | `#1C1B1F` | the spark on light backgrounds, one-colour mark |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | ring on red or dark |

Amber is never used on white (too little contrast); on light backgrounds the spark is Ink.
In the app UI, colours still come from Material dynamic colour; the brand red is the fallback seed.

## Clear space and minimum size
- Clear space on every side: one spark diameter (40 on the 256 grid).
- Minimum size: 16 px / 16 dp. Below 24 px use the one-colour version (the amber spark loses contrast).
- Don't: rotate the mark, move or resize the spark, close the gap, add outlines, shadows or gradients,
  put amber on white, or set the ring in amber.

## Motion: splash (1000 ms + 380 ms exit)
| Time | What moves | Curve |
|---|---|---|
| 0-600 ms | ring draws clockwise from its start (trim 0 to 1) | fast-out-slow-in |
| 0-640 ms | whole mark swings from -110 deg to 0 | fast-out-slow-in |
| 420-720 ms | spark pops in on the ring's path (scale 0 to 1) | overshoot |
| 640-1000 ms | ripple leaves the spark (scale 1 to 2.6, alpha 0.9 to 0) | linear-out-slow-in |
| 1000-1380 ms | exit: mark grows to 1.35 and fades, red background dissolves into the app | emphasized |

The animated drawable's resting values are the final frame, so Android 11 and older (no animated splash) show
the finished logo. `splash-preview.html` replays the same choreography in a browser; `splash-preview.mp4`,
`.gif` and `splash-storyboard.png` are recordings of it.
